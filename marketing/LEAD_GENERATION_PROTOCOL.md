# Min-Max V1 Lead Generation Protocol
**Founder-Led Manual Outreach Strategy (Zero Automation)**

## 1. Objective
To secure the first cohort of 50 active users through direct, high-touch interaction led by the founder. This protocol ensures zero technical debt and allows the founder to gather direct qualitative feedback from elite fitness professionals and early adopters.

## 2. Core Execution Owner
- **Haroon (Founder)**: Responsible for all direct outreach, platform demonstrations, and community engagement.

## 3. Daily Quotas for Haroon
To ensure consistent growth without automation, Haroon will commit to the following daily manual targets:
- **15 Direct Outreach Messages**: Personalized, high-value invitations to personal trainers and gym owners via Instagram, LinkedIn, or direct SMS.
- **5 On-Site Demonstrations**: Physically visiting local gyms or training studios to demonstrate the 120Hz chart performance and fluid logging on a device.
- **3 "White Glove" Onboarding Sessions**: Manually guiding users through their first unit preference setup and database seeding to ensure a 100% success rate.
- **5 Feedback Interviews**: Conducting 5-minute deep-dives with active users to identify V1.1 feature priorities.

## 4. Platform Constraints (The "No-Automation" Rule)
> [!IMPORTANT]
> To preserve the engineering integrity of the V1 launch and maintain the founder's connection to the user base, the following are strictly prohibited:
> - NO CRM integrations (Hubspot, Salesforce, etc.).
> - NO Automated email sequences or bulk mailing services.
> - NO Lead-gen bots or scraping scripts.
> - NO Third-party growth agencies.

All lead tracking must be handled by Haroon personally using a manual spreadsheet or a simple physical notebook.

## 5. Script Guidelines for Haroon
- **Value Proposition**: "I built Min-Max because existing apps were too slow and didn't handle professional-grade analytics offline. It's built for 120Hz performance and transactional data integrity."
- **Closing**: "I'm personally onboarding the first 50 users. Can I send you a private TestFlight link today?"

## 6. Success Metrics
- **Personal Conversion Rate**: Percentage of Haroon's outreaches that result in a completed workout log.
- **Founder-User Proximity**: Qualitative insights gathered per week to inform the product roadmap.
